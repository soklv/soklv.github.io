The fixed rules (v1.6)

1. Universe: BTC, ETH, XAUT, RQQQ, RSPY, RNVDA, US30, DE40, JP225, HK50, EURUSD, USDJPY, XAGUSD, UKOUSD — real daily closes from public exchange APIs (no account, no keys): spot candles for the tokenized assets, CFD quote history for the indices, forex, silver and oil.

2. One evaluation per closed day, in the morning issue (~07:00 Riga). No intraday moves, no discretionary calls.

3. BUY 10% of remaining cash when the 7-day return is above +1% and the close is above its 30-day average; never more than 50% of equity in one asset; skip if less than 10 USDT free.

4. SELL half a position when its 7-day return drops below −8% (rules v1.6, 2026-11-06: cut trigger deepened from −5% by the full-history tuning grid — +4.1% final equity and 6.2% vs 7.6% max drawdown, dominating every control window). Otherwise hold. Fee 0.1% on every fill. Rules v1.1: a position worth under 1 USDT is closed in full instead of being halved forever — real exchanges reject sub-minimum orders.

Honest limits

Daily-close fills only: no intraday prices, no slippage, no partial-fill quirks, funding rates not modelled. A position under 1 USDT would not even be accepted by a real exchange — rules v1.1 closes it in full. CFD positions are priced from the buy-side daily quote; the real spread and overnight financing are not modelled, and the flat 0.1% paper fee overstates a real CFD’s cost — deliberately conservative. No leverage anywhere: every CFD position is a 1x long, like a spot position. Real trading would differ. That is exactly why this desk is labelled paper everywhere.

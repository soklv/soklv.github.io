About sok.lv

What this site is, what it is for, and how every number on it is made.

What sok.lv is

sok.lv is a small, independent site made in Riga, Latvia. It is free to read — no account, no paywall, nothing to install. Two things live here. First, two learning projects: ELI5, short illustrated explanations of big ideas, and Book850, a novel told in Ogden’s Basic English — 850 chapters, one new word per chapter, each with its own video. Both projects are paired with videos on the KraSer YouTube channel.

Second, a small daily desk. Every morning it publishes two pages: the real sky — planet positions, the moon’s phase, sunrise and sunset for Latvia, and a reading for each of the twelve signs — and the markets digest, a one-page summary of crypto, tokenized gold and stocks, central-bank rates and the day’s macro calendar. A third desk, the paper-trading experiment, runs a simulated portfolio on real daily prices, with fixed rules and every decision logged. Every issue is kept: each day gets its own page in the archive.

How the numbers are made

Every number on this site is computed or taken from a named public source — nothing is copied from another horoscope or finance site. Sky positions come from ephemeris math of the kind planetariums use, computed fresh for each page’s date; “retrograde” is measured by comparing the body’s longitude on consecutive days, not taken from folklore. Market prices, yields and rates come from public endpoints — Bitget, US Treasury, the New York Fed, the ECB and Yahoo Finance’s public feed — and every markets page is stamped with the exact minute of its snapshot.

The plain-English readings — the twelve horoscope lines and the What happened / Why / Tomorrow market notes — are written daily by an AI from those same computed numbers. Where a claim is astrology rather than astronomy, the page says so; where a page is about finance, it says that nothing on it is investment advice. The site’s bias is to show the machinery: what is a fact layer and what is a story layer is labelled on each page.

Who is behind it

sok.lv is written and maintained by the person behind the KraSer YouTube channel, with help from the same AI tools that write the daily readings. It carries no third-party advertising beyond Google AdSense, sells nothing, and does not collect anything beyond what the privacy policy describes.

The fastest way to reach the desk is a comment or message on the KraSer YouTube channel — real questions from real readers are the best bug reports.

What it is not

It is not a news agency, a financial adviser or an astrology service. It is a small, honest desk that computes the sky, reads the tape, and writes down both in plain language — every day, in the open, kept in an archive you can check against yesterday’s page.

What this page is — and isn’t

The fact layer: geocentric ecliptic longitudes computed from the same class of ephemeris math planetariums use, for today’s date. The zodiac here is a coordinate system — 360° of sky cut into twelve 30° signs.

The story layer: the twelve readings above, written by an AI from those same positions — the oldest use of this data, and the part where astrology’s claims stay unproven. Numbers are the fact, readings are the story; both are computed here, not copied.

What this page is — and isn’t

These are the positions astronomy itself agrees on: geocentric ecliptic longitudes computed from the same class of ephemeris math planetariums use, for today’s date. The zodiac here is a coordinate system — 360° of sky cut into twelve 30° signs.

What it isn’t: a prediction. Whether the sky’s clock says anything about your week is the oldest open question in astrology — and the honest answer starts from knowing where things actually are. That part is on this page.

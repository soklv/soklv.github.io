A small corner of the internet where learning stays simple. Two sites live here — both free, both made to be read in plain English — and a daily desk writes two short data pages: today’s real sky — horoscope included — and today’s markets.

Projects

ELI5 · eli5.sok.lv

Big ideas explained like you're five — astronomy, linguistics, AI, crypto, biochemistry and more. Short illustrated stories, each paired with a video on the KraSer channel.

Book850 · be850.sok.lv

The book begins with one word. 850 chapters, 850 words, one story — told only with Ogden's Basic English, adding a single new word per chapter. Every chapter comes with its video, so the book teaches itself.

KraSer on YouTube · video

Video home for both projects: every ELI5 story is paired with a video, and every Book850 chapter comes with its own.

Daily

The Real Sky — today · horoscope

Where the planets actually are right now: every position computed from ephemeris — the same math planetariums use. Moon phase and illumination, next full moon, sunrise and sunset for Riga. Then a short daily reading for each of the 12 signs, computed from those same positions. The sky itself: no predictions — just the clock.

Markets digest — today · finance

Crypto, tokenized gold, tokenized S&P 500 and Nasdaq in one small table: last price and 24h/7d/30d moves — pulled from a public exchange API and stamped with the exact minute.

Every issue is kept: the archive — one page per day, sky and horoscope plus markets, frozen at the morning run.

Paper trading

Paper trading desk · experiment

A simulated portfolio on real daily prices from a public exchange API: fixed rules, a 0.1% fee, one decision per day — and every trade committed to the git history of this site. Paper money only, nowhere near investment advice.

Everything is free to read and watch — no sign-up, nothing to install. More may take root here later.

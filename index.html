This domain is the home of Book850 — a novel that teaches itself English. Two side entrances keep the rest of the house: ELI5 explainers for big ideas made small, and a daily desk that writes the real sky and the markets every morning, in plain English.

The book

Book850 · be850.sok.lv

One story, 850 chapters long. The first chapter is told with three words of Ogden’s Basic English; every next chapter adds exactly one new word. By the last chapter you have met the whole working vocabulary of English — without ever opening a textbook. Every chapter comes with its own video, so the book teaches itself.

Every day

The Real Sky — today · horoscope

Where the planets actually are right now: every position computed from ephemeris — the same math planetariums use. Moon phase and illumination, next full moon, sunrise and sunset for Latvia. Then a short daily reading for each of the 12 signs, computed from those same positions. The sky itself: no predictions — just the clock.

Markets digest — today · finance

Crypto, tokenized gold, tokenized S&P 500 and Nasdaq in one small table: last price and 24h/7d/30d moves — plus 10Y Treasury, Fed and ECB rates, the FOMC calendar, market headlines and a plain-English What happened / Why / Tomorrow. Every number from a public endpoint, stamped with the exact minute.

Every issue is kept: the archive — one page per day, sky and horoscope plus markets, frozen at the morning run.

Also here

Paper trading desk · experiment

A simulated portfolio on real daily prices from a public exchange API: fixed rules, a 0.1% fee, one decision per day — and every trade committed to the git history of this site. Paper money only, nowhere near investment advice.

How it all is made · about

Every number here is computed or taken from a named public source — sky positions from ephemeris math computed fresh for each day, market prices from public endpoints stamped with the exact minute. The plain-English readings are written daily by an AI from those same numbers, and the pages label what is fact and what is story. Made in Riga, free to read, kept in a public archive you can check against yesterday’s page.

Everything is free to read and watch — no sign-up, nothing to install. More may take root here later.

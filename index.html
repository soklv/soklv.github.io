The language behind the book

Basic English is Charles Kay Ogden’s 1930 list of 850 words picked to carry everyday English: 600 things, 100 operations and 150 qualities. With them you can say most of what a person says in a day — simply, correctly, without a dictionary. Book850 uses exactly this list as its whole vocabulary, so nothing in the book runs ahead of the reader.

How the book teaches itself

Chapter one is told with three words from the list. Every next chapter adds exactly one new word and wears it in with the old ones — by repetition, not by rules. Each chapter has its own page and its own video, and the whole 850-word list is open on the site: at any moment you know exactly how much English you already own.

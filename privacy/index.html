Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what data the sok.lv website (including its subdomains eli5.sok.lv and be850.sok.lv) collects and why. The short version: the site is free to read, needs no account, and collects as little as possible — standard analytics and, if you accept them, advertising cookies.

What we collect

  • Analytics data. We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and understand which pages are read. Google Analytics collects an anonymised part of your IP address, the pages you view, the referring page, and general browser and device information. No names, no emails, no profiles that identify you personally.
  • Server logs. Like almost every website, the hosting keeps short-lived technical logs (IP address, requested page, timestamp) for security and troubleshooting.
  • Nothing else. No registration, no newsletters, no forms, no sold or shared personal data — there is simply nothing to sell.

Cookies

Cookies are small files a site stores in your browser. This site uses them for two purposes:

  • Measurement cookies set by Google Analytics (such as _ga), which remember that the same browser returned, so visit counts make sense.
  • Advertising cookies, set by Google and its partners once advertising is shown on the site — see the next section.

Google AdSense and advertising

This site shows advertising served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising at any time on Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. For readers in the European Economic Area, ads are served in line with Google's consent requirements: where required, you will be asked for consent before personalised advertising cookies are set.

Google's own privacy explanation lives at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union (this site is operated from Latvia), you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data relating to you, to object to or restrict processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Latvia: Data State Inspectorate, dvi.gov.lv). Because this site collects no identifying data, in practice there is usually nothing to look up — but the rights stand. To exercise them, contact the operator of the sok.lv domain.

Retention

Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics according to its standard retention settings; hosting logs are kept only for a short technical period. Content on the site (articles, daily sky and markets pages) is published data, not personal data.

Changes

If the site adds a service that changes what is collected, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. The current version is always the one published here.
